ABNORMAL product visual mapping
================================

The product cards and product detail pages now use one shared visual resolver:
- DRIP products keep their previous ProductArtwork + product.logo overlay logic.
- Every non-DRIP built-in product gets an explicit, slug-matched asset in
  src/components/product-visual.tsx.
- Non-DRIP product.logo fields were removed from the catalog, so no old logo
  overlay or generic ABN fallback can be applied to those products.
